# Scenario 3 — "Ulubiona scena" (Culture Lovers, Follow Venue + Push)

> Part of the B2C promotional scenarios set. Overview, comparison and rollout: [00-overview.md](00-overview.md).
> Lead feature: **Follow a venue + push notifications** · Channel type: **organic / community-led** (venues, Facebook groups, local media)

## 1. Name

- **Internal name:** `ulubiona-scena`
- **Headline (PL):** „Twoje ulubione miejsce da Ci znać pierwsze.”
- **EN:** "Your favourite venue will tell you first."

## 2. Target segment and insight

**Culture enthusiasts, 25–45.** They go to the theatre, galleries, jazz clubs and concerts regularly, but they keep finding out about the premiere, the vernissage or the one-off concert **after it's sold out or over**, because the venue's post got buried by the Facebook algorithm.

## 3. Core idea

Instead of chasing users ourselves, we let **Poznań's cultural venues** do the talking. Each partner venue tells its own audience: "Obserwuj nas w Wydarzce — dostaniesz powiadomienie, gdy dodamy nowe wydarzenie." Venues get a channel that isn't controlled by the Facebook algorithm, and users get a calm, curated heads-up only from places they chose. Community Scout is the secondary hook: "Brakuje Twojej ulubionej sceny? Zgłoś ją."

## 4. User journey

1. **First contact:** a regular at a small theatre sees a post on the venue's Facebook or IG, a note in the printed monthly programme, or a table card at the gallery café: "Obserwuj nas w Wydarzce".
2. **Web visit → install:** the link or QR opens the **venue's public web page** on wydarzka.dev with upcoming events. The **"Obserwuj w aplikacji"** button there leads to the store.
3. **First open:** the app opens on the map. The user searches for or opens the venue from an event detail and taps **Follow**.
4. **Aha moment:** the system prompt for **push notifications** appears **only at that moment**, with a pre-permission screen explaining "Powiadomimy Cię tylko o nowych wydarzeniach w miejscach, które obserwujesz". Opt-in only. A few days later the first push lands: a new event at *their* venue, before the Facebook post.
5. **Return visit:** push → **event detail** → **Save**. While in the app they see other venues nearby on the map and follow 1–2 more. That's the Follow → Push → Return loop.

## 5. Channels and formats

| Format | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Venue partner kit (post template, Story template, A6 table card with QR, one line for the printed programme) | 15–20 Poznań venues: independent theatres, galleries, jazz/indie clubs, cultural centres | Free for venues. The venue publishes from its own account, so it isn't disguised promotion |
| 30–45s video "Za kulisami" (behind the scenes) | Posted by the venue and by Wydarzka (collab post) | Venue manager in their own words. It's the venue's own voice, clearly a partnership, labelled as a collab |
| Facebook group posts | Poznań culture groups ("Kultura w Poznaniu", theatre and gig groups) | As the Wydarzka page, **within group rules / with admin approval**, framed as a useful monthly "premiery i wernisaże" listing, not an ad |
| Local media pitch | Culture sections of local portals and city culture guides | Story angle: "Poznańskie sceny omijają algorytm Facebooka" |
| Secondary: Community Scout call | End of every group post | „Brakuje miejsca? Zgłoś je w aplikacji.” |

## 6. Sample creative (Polish)

### A. 40s video script — "Za kulisami" (venue collab)

| Time | Scene | On-screen text / VO |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4s | An empty small theatre, lights coming up on stage. The camera slowly moves between the seats. | Text: **Ile razy dowiedziałeś(-aś) się o premierze, gdy było już po wszystkim?** |
| 4–15s | The venue manager sits on the edge of the stage and talks to camera. | VO (manager): „Wrzucamy post, a potem widzi go może co dziesiąta osoba, która nas obserwuje. Ludzie piszą do nas po spektaklu: czemu nic nie wiedziałam?” |
| 15–25s | Screen recording: venue page in Wydarzka → tap Follow → push notification lands: „Nowe wydarzenie: [Nazwa sceny] — premiera »…«”. | VO: „Teraz, kiedy dodajemy nowe wydarzenie, osoby, które nas obserwują w Wydarzce, dostają powiadomienie. Bez algorytmu po drodze.” |
| 25–35s | Rehearsal, the audience coming in, applause. | VO: „Jeśli lubisz do nas przychodzić — obserwuj nas tam. Będziesz wiedzieć pierwszy(-a).” |
| 35–40s | End card with the venue logo × Wydarzka logo | **Obserwuj [Nazwa sceny] w Wydarzce.** Small print: „Materiał powstał we współpracy z Wydarzka.” |

### B. Venue post / Facebook group post

**Venue's own post (template):**
> Mamy prośbę 🎭
> Facebook pokazuje nasze posty coraz mniejszej liczbie osób — a potem słyszymy „szkoda, że nie wiedziałem o tym spektaklu”.
> Dlatego jesteśmy teraz w Wydarzce. Kliknij „Obserwuj”, a dostaniesz powiadomienie za każdym razem, gdy dodamy nowe wydarzenie. Tylko od nas, tylko wtedy, gdy coś się dzieje.
> 👉 [link do strony miejsca na wydarzka.dev]

**Monthly Facebook group post (Wydarzka page, with admin approval):**
> Premiery, wernisaże i koncerty w Poznaniu — październik 🍂
> Zebraliśmy w jednym miejscu, co warto zapisać w kalendarzu:
> • [data] — [wydarzenie], [miejsce]
> • [data] — [wydarzenie], [miejsce]
> • …
> Pełna lista z mapą: [link do strony kategorii na wydarzka.dev]
> Jeśli chcesz dostawać info o nowych wydarzeniach z konkretnego miejsca — możesz je obserwować w aplikacji.
> Brakuje Waszej ulubionej sceny? Dajcie znać w komentarzu albo zgłoście ją w aplikacji 💜

## 7. Timing and city

- **City:** **Poznań** first (partnerships are built face to face). Start venue outreach in **launch week**, publish venue posts from **week 3–4** once each venue has ≥ 3 upcoming events in the app, and run it through month 3.
- **Moment:** the start of the autumn theatre and gallery season (October–November) is when venues publish their new programme.

## 8. Budget

**200–400 PLN total**

| Item | PLN |
|---|---|
| A6 table cards with QR for venues (1,000 pcs) | 120–200 |
| Travel and coffee for venue meetings | 50–100 |
| Optional small boost of the best venue collab video to 25–45 culture interests in Poznań | 0–100 |

## 9. KPIs

| Metric | Target (3 months) |
|---|---|
| **Primary: venue follows** | 1,500 follows from ~700 users (≥ 2 follows/user) |
| Push opt-in rate among users who followed a venue | ≥ 60% |
| D30 retention of followers vs. non-followers | Followers ≥ 2× non-followers (e.g. 25% vs. 12%) |
| *Supporting:* active partner venues | 15 venues posting at least once |
| *Supporting:* Scout submissions from this audience | 40+ |

## 10. Risks and mitigation

- **Most likely failure:** venues agree in principle but never post. Or they post once, followers get no push because the venue's events aren't updated in Wydarzka, and the promise ("dostaniesz powiadomienie") is broken.
- **Mitigation:** pick venues whose programme we can keep up to date ourselves (import or manual entry by the team) so a push actually goes out within 2 weeks of the post. Give venues a **ready-to-publish kit** and a specific publish date. Follow up personally, and send each venue a monthly "you gained X followers" note so they keep going.
