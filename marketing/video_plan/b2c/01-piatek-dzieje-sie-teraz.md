# Scenario 1 — "Piątek 18:00" (Friday Night, Happening Now)

> Part of the B2C promotional scenarios set. Overview, comparison and rollout: [00-overview.md](00-overview.md).
> Lead feature: **Happening Now** ("Dzieje się teraz") · Channel type: **paid social**

## 1. Name

- **Internal name:** `piatek-1800`
- **Headline (PL):** „Co dziś wieczorem? Sprawdź, co dzieje się teraz.”
- **EN:** "What's on tonight? See what's happening right now."

## 2. Target segment and insight

**Urban explorers, 18–35.** Every Friday around 18:00 the group chat goes "so what are we doing?" and it ends in the same bar again, not because nothing's on but because nobody wants to dig through five Facebook pages, Instagram stories and a ticketing site to find out.

## 3. Core idea

We catch people during that Friday-evening moment of indecision with short, native-feeling videos that show the whole answer in one tap: open the map, tap "Dzieje się teraz", see what starts in the next few hours within walking distance. The ads run only Thursday–Saturday, 15:00–21:00, so they show up while people are actually deciding.

## 4. User journey

1. **First contact:** a 15s Reel/TikTok ad in the feed on Friday at 17:30 shows a group chat stuck on "no to co robimy?" and cuts to the map.
2. **Install:** the CTA goes to the App Store or Google Play (Meta app install campaign or TikTok app promotion). Web fallback for desktop: `wydarzka.dev/poznan`.
3. **First open:** the app **opens straight on the map** with no login. The user can opt in to location; if they say no, the map centres on central Poznań.
4. **Aha moment:** the user taps the **"Dzieje się teraz"** filter and pins for events in the next 0–8 hours appear nearby. They narrow it with the **distance filter (1–2 km)**, open an **event detail** and tap **Nawiguj**.
5. **Return visit:** the next Friday they get a retargeting Story ("Znowu piątek. Zobacz, co dzieje się teraz”). They open the app and use **Dzieje się teraz** again, and it becomes a habit. Push is optional; we don't rely on it here.

## 5. Channels and formats

| Format | Placement | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 15s vertical video (2 variants) | Instagram Reels + Stories, TikTok In-Feed | Main creative; test "group chat" vs. "screen recording" hooks |
| 6s bumper | IG Stories | Map with pins lighting up, plus the headline |
| Static Story | IG/FB Stories retargeting | For people who watched ≥50% of the video but didn't install |
| Organic repost | Wydarzka TikTok/IG | The same videos posted organically on Friday at 17:00 as the "Dziś wieczorem w Poznaniu" series |

**Targeting:** Poznań + 15 km radius, ages 18–35, broad interests (let the algorithm optimise). **Schedule:** Thursday 15:00 to Saturday 21:00 only.

## 6. Sample creative (Polish)

### A. 15s Reel/TikTok script — "Grupa na czacie"

| Time | Scene | On-screen text / VO |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2s | Close-up of a phone in someone's hand on a tram. Group chat messages pop up: „no to co dziś?”, „nie wiem, wy wybierzcie”, „znowu to samo miejsce? 😅” | Text: **Piątek, 18:47. Znasz to.** |
| 2–5s | Thumb swipes to Wydarzka. The Poznań map opens right away, with violet pins spread around the Old Town and Jeżyce. | VO (calm, a little ironic): „Zamiast pytać na grupie…” |
| 5–9s | Tap „Dzieje się teraz”. Pins filter down: a jazz concert, an open-air cinema, a board game night, a vinyl DJ set. | Text: **Wszystko, co zaczyna się w najbliższych godzinach. Na jednej mapie.** |
| 9–12s | Event detail opens → tap „Udostępnij” → it lands in the group chat. Reply: „idę 🙌” | VO: „…po prostu sprawdź, co dzieje się teraz.” |
| 12–15s | End card on a soft violet gradient with the logo | **Wydarzka. Odkryj swoje miasto.** Button: „Pobierz za darmo” |

> Creative rule: no alcohol in frame and no drinks in hand. Polish law (ustawa o wychowaniu w trzeźwości) restricts alcohol advertising. Show music, people and places instead.

### B. Ad copy (Meta / TikTok)

**Primary text:**
> Piątek wieczór, a plan dalej „jakoś to będzie”? 🙃
> Otwórz Wydarzkę, kliknij „Dzieje się teraz” i zobacz koncerty, wystawy, pokazy i spotkania, które startują w ciągu kilku najbliższych godzin — blisko Ciebie.
> Bez zakładania konta. Po prostu mapa i Twoje miasto.

**Headline:** Co dziś wieczorem w Poznaniu?
**CTA button:** Zainstaluj

**Retargeting Story (static):**
> Znowu piątek.
> Zobacz, co dzieje się teraz → Wydarzka

## 7. Timing and city

- **City:** start in **Poznań** (launch city with the densest event data). Copy it to the 2nd city only once CPI is below 6 PLN.
- **When:** launch week through month 3. Run it every weekend and make the flights heavier on weekends with bigger local happenings.

## 8. Budget

**2,000–2,400 PLN total over 3 months (~700–800 PLN/month)**

| Item | PLN |
|---|---|
| Meta (IG Reels/Stories) app install campaign | 1,200–1,400 |
| TikTok app promotion | 500–600 |
| Retargeting (IG/FB Stories) | 300–400 |
| Production | 0, shot on a phone with screen recordings in-house |

## 9. KPIs

| Metric | Target (3 months, Poznań) |
|---|---|
| **Primary: paid installs** | 400–550 (CPI 4–6 PLN) |
| "Dzieje się teraz" filter used in the first session | ≥ 45% of new users from this campaign |
| D7 retention | ≥ 18% (a return the next weekend is the real signal) |

## 10. Risks and mitigation

- **Most likely failure:** in off-peak hours, or on weekday-like Fridays, "Dzieje się teraz" shows only a handful of pins. The aha moment turns into "empty map", and people uninstall.
- **Mitigation:** before each flight, check the Poznań event count for Friday 18:00–02:00. Only run the ad set when there are **≥ 25 events** in the 0–8h window within the city. If the count is thin, switch the creative to the **"Ten weekend"** preset instead of pausing. Feed the gaps through venue outreach ([03](03-ulubiona-scena-obserwuj.md)) and Community Scout.
