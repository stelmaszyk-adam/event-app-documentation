# Scenario 5 — "Nowy w mieście" (Newcomers & Tourists, SEO Event Pages)

> Part of the B2C promotional scenarios set. Overview, comparison and rollout: [00-overview.md](00-overview.md).
> Lead feature: **public SEO event pages** on wydarzka.dev · Channel type: **search (organic SEO + small Google Ads) + travel/relocation content partners**

## 1. Name

- **Internal name:** `nowy-w-miescie`
- **Headline (PL):** „Jesteś tu od niedawna? Zobacz, czym żyje Poznań w ten weekend.”
- **EN:** "New here? See what Poznań is up to this weekend."

## 2. Target segment and insight

**Newcomers (people relocating for work, Erasmus/international students, remote workers) and weekend tourists.** They don't follow local venues or local Facebook groups yet, so they do what everyone does in a new place: **google "co robić w Poznaniu w weekend"**. What they get back is outdated listicles, "top 10 attractions" and nothing about what's actually on *this* weekend.

## 3. Core idea

We win the search moment with pages that are **current by design**: public, indexable city, category and event pages on wydarzka.dev, backed by a small set of evergreen-but-live guide pages ("Co robić w Poznaniu w ten weekend", "Darmowe wydarzenia w Poznaniu") that pull from real listings. A modest Google Ads budget covers the highest-intent queries while organic ranking builds. Content partners (relocation groups, travel blogs, short-stay hosts) link to those pages. The app is offered as the natural next step: "zapisz i nawiguj w aplikacji".

## 4. User journey

1. **First contact:** a Google search for "co robić w Poznaniu w weekend", "koncerty Poznań listopad" or "wydarzenia Poznań dziś" shows an organic result or search ad for a Wydarzka city/category page. Or a link in a relocation guide or a host's welcome message.
2. **Web visit:** the **city/category page** (e.g. `/poznan/music`) opens with this weekend's events, then the **event page** with time, address, description and map. It's fully usable without the app.
3. **Install:** on the event page the **smart banner** ("Zapisz w aplikacji" / "Nawiguj w aplikacji") leads to the store.
4. **Aha moment:** the app opens on the **map** of the city they're in. The newcomer sees **how much is happening around their flat or hotel**, uses the **"Ten weekend"** preset and taps **Nawiguj** (Google/Apple Maps) to get there without knowing the city.
5. **Return visit:** a newcomer comes back the next weekend with **"Jutro" / "Ten weekend"**. For tourists, the return is the web page itself: next time they visit any of the 7 cities, Google sends them to the same kind of page.

## 5. Channels and formats

| Format | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Live guide pages (5–8) | wydarzka.dev: "Co robić w Poznaniu w ten weekend", "Darmowe wydarzenia w Poznaniu", "Poznań z dziećmi — ten weekend", "Koncerty w Poznaniu w [miesiąc]" | Auto-updated lists plus a short editorial intro. Target long-tail queries |
| Google Search Ads | Exact/phrase: "co robić w poznaniu", "wydarzenia poznań weekend", "koncerty poznań dziś" | Small daily cap. Landing = matching live page, never the homepage |
| Relocation/expat communities | Relocation Facebook groups, Erasmus/ESN section newsletters, HR onboarding packs of Poznań employers (via LinkedIn outreach) | A useful link, following group rules |
| Short-stay hosts and hostels | QR welcome card: "Co dziś w okolicy?" | Free printable PDF for hosts |
| Travel/city bloggers | Guest links: "aktualne wydarzenia — zobacz tutaj" in their Poznań guides | Link exchange / free listing of their city walks. Sponsored posts labelled as such |
| Google Business Profile + structured data check | Event rich results | Technical SEO hygiene (see MARKETING-B2C.md §2.1) |

## 6. Sample creative (Polish)

### A. Google Search Ads (RSA)

**Headlines (≤ 30 characters):**
1. Co robić w Poznaniu w weekend
2. Wydarzenia w Poznaniu dziś
3. Koncerty, wystawy, spotkania
4. Wszystko na jednej mapie
5. Sprawdź, co dzieje się blisko
6. Bez rejestracji. Za darmo.

**Descriptions (≤ 90 characters):**
1. Aktualne wydarzenia w Poznaniu na ten weekend — z godzinami, adresem i mapą dojazdu.
2. Nowy w mieście? Zobacz koncerty, wystawy i spotkania obok Ciebie. Zapisz i nawiguj.

**Sitelinks:** Ten weekend · Darmowe wydarzenia · Z dziećmi · Koncerty

### B. Intro text for the "Co robić w Poznaniu w ten weekend" guide page + host welcome card

**Guide page intro (above the live event list):**
> ## Co robić w Poznaniu w ten weekend?
> Jeśli jesteś w Poznaniu od niedawna — albo tylko na kilka dni — nie musisz przekopywać się przez dziesiątki profili i starych artykułów. Poniżej znajdziesz wydarzenia, które naprawdę odbywają się w ten weekend: koncerty, wystawy, spektakle, targi, bieganie w parku i spotkania dla nowych w mieście.
> Lista aktualizuje się na bieżąco. Każde wydarzenie ma godzinę, adres i mapę dojazdu. Chcesz mieć je pod ręką? Zapisz w aplikacji Wydarzka i nawiguj jednym kliknięciem.

**Host/hostel welcome card (A6, QR):**
> **Co dziś w okolicy?** 🗺️
> Witaj w Poznaniu! Zeskanuj kod i zobacz na mapie, co dzieje się dziś i w ten weekend — kilka kroków od miejsca, w którym mieszkasz.
> Bez logowania. Bez szukania po grupach.
> *Wydarzka — odkryj swoje miasto.*

> Optional: an English variant of the host card for international guests, if the web pages are served in English.

## 7. Timing and city

- **City:** **Poznań** first for the guide pages and ads. Guide pages for all 7 cities go live as soon as each has enough listings, because SEO needs a 6–10 week head start. **Kraków and Wrocław** (highest tourist search volume) are worth opening early on the organic side even before active promotion there.
- **When:** guide pages and technical SEO in **launch week**. Google Ads from **week 1** (a steady baseline). Community and host outreach in **months 2–3**, once the pages are indexed and ranking.

## 8. Budget

**700–1,000 PLN total over 3 months**

| Item | PLN |
|---|---|
| Google Search Ads (~200–300 PLN/month, capped CPC) | 600–850 |
| Printing A6 host welcome cards (500 pcs) | 80–120 |
| Content writing | 0, in-house |
| Outreach | 0, time only |

## 9. KPIs

| Metric | Target (3 months) |
|---|---|
| **Primary: organic + paid sessions on city/category/event pages (Poznań)** | 8,000 sessions (organic ≥ 50% by month 3) |
| Web → app install rate (smart banner) | 3–5% of event-page sessions → ~250–400 installs |
| Top-10 Google positions for tracked long-tail queries | ≥ 10 queries in top 10 by month 3 |
| *Supporting:* Google Ads CTR / CPC | CTR ≥ 6%, CPC ≤ 1.50 PLN |

## 10. Risks and mitigation

- **Most likely failure:** SEO is slow. Pages aren't indexed or don't rank within the 3-month window, and Google Ads alone bring expensive traffic that bounces from thin pages.
- **Mitigation:** submit sitemaps and check indexing in Search Console in week 1. Make guide pages **useful even with few events** (editorial intro, category links, "Jutro" and "Ten weekend" sections). Put the Ads budget only on the 10–15 queries with the best conversion. Get early backlinks from relocation guides and host cards so the pages don't rely only on ranking.
