# Scenario 2 — "Pierwszy tydzień" (Students' First Week, Map-First Discovery)

> Part of the B2C promotional scenarios set. Overview, comparison and rollout: [00-overview.md](00-overview.md).
> Lead feature: **map-first discovery** · Channel type: **offline / guerrilla** (real-world trigger: start of the academic year)

## 1. Name

- **Internal name:** `pierwszy-tydzien`
- **Headline (PL):** „Nowe miasto? Mapę masz już w kieszeni.”
- **EN:** "New city? You've already got the map in your pocket."

## 2. Target segment and insight

**Students, 18–25, especially first-years and people who've just moved to Poznań.** In their first weeks they don't know the city, don't have "their" places yet and don't know what's free or cheap. They want to go out and meet people but have no idea where to start, and they're nervous about picking wrong.

## 3. Core idea

We borrow the most recognisable offline format in Polish student life, the **tear-off flyer on the dorm notice board** ("zrywki"), and turn it into a playful guerrilla ad. Instead of "Pokój do wzięcia", the flyer says "Miasto do wzięcia". Each tear-off strip has a QR code that opens the app straight on the city map. It runs during the first 2–3 weeks of October, the one moment when a whole cohort is actively looking for "what's out there".

## 4. User journey

1. **First contact:** a first-year sees a violet tear-off flyer on the dorm or faculty board, or gets a sticker at a student government welcome event.
2. **Install / web visit:** they scan the QR (`wydarzka.dev/poznan?utm_source=offline&utm_campaign=pierwszy-tydzien&utm_content=<location>`), which lands on the **Poznań city page on web**. The smart banner sends them to the store; if they don't install, they can still browse the web pages.
3. **First open:** the app **opens on the map** with no login and no tutorial. Pins appear all over the city, colour-coded by category.
4. **Aha moment:** they pan the map around their own dorm or faculty and find things they didn't know were **a 10-minute walk away**. They use the **distance filter (0.5–1 km)** and the **map/list toggle** to go through them. Colour-coded **categories** (education, sport & fitness, music) help them find their thing.
5. **Return visit:** at the weekend they open the map with the **"Ten weekend" (This weekend)** preset to plan an outing with new friends from the dorm.

## 5. Channels and formats

| Format | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A4 tear-off flyer "Miasto do wzięcia" (10 QR strips) | Dorm and faculty notice boards (UAM, PUT, UEP, UMP, UAP) | **Only with the administration's or student government's permission.** One QR per location for attribution |
| A3 poster "Zanim poznasz ludzi, poznaj miejsca" | Faculty boards, student clubs, bike/café spots near campuses | Same QR logic |
| Sticker with QR (5×5 cm) | Handed out at welcome events or left at partner cafés | Never stuck on public property (that's vandalism) |
| Partnership with student governments (samorządy) | Newsletter mention, post in first-year Facebook groups ("UAM 2026/2027 — pierwszy rok") | Exchange: we add their welcome events to the map for free |
| 15s TikTok (organic) | Wydarzka TikTok, shared by student gov accounts | "Documenting" the tear-off flyers around campus |

## 6. Sample creative (Polish)

### A. Tear-off flyer copy (A4)

> **MIASTO DO WZIĘCIA** 🏙️
>
> Świeżo po przeprowadzce? Nie znasz jeszcze nikogo ani niczego?
> Spokojnie. Koncerty, spotkania, darmowe wykłady, bieganie w parku, jam session w piwnicy — wszystko jest już na mapie.
>
> Bez rejestracji. Otwierasz i widzisz, co dzieje się obok akademika.
>
> **Oderwij karteczkę. Zeskanuj. Odkryj Poznań.**
>
> *Tear-off strips (×10, rotated):* `QR` · „Miasto do wzięcia → Wydarzka”

### B. 15s TikTok script — "Zrywki"

| Time | Scene | On-screen text / VO |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Handheld shot of a cluttered dorm board: flat ads, "sprzedam rower", lost key notices. The camera stops on a violet flyer that reads MIASTO DO WZIĘCIA. | Text: **Pierwszy tydzień w Poznaniu be like:** |
| 3–6s | A hand tears off a strip and scans the QR. | VO: „Szukasz pokoju? Nie. Szukasz, co robić.” |
| 6–11s | Screen recording: the map opens instantly, zooms around the dorm, pins light up (a board game night in a café, a free lecture, a running club in Park Cytadela). | Text: **Wszystko, co dzieje się blisko Ciebie. Na jednej mapie.** |
| 11–15s | Back to the board: almost every strip is torn off. End card. | **Wydarzka. Odkryj swoje miasto.** Text: „Znajdziesz nas na tablicy 😉” |

**Post caption:**
> Zostawiliśmy kilka karteczek na tablicach w akademikach. Jeśli jedna została — jest Twoja 😄
> Pierwszy rok? Otwórz mapę i zobacz, co dzieje się w Twojej okolicy. #Poznań #pierwszyrok #studia

## 7. Timing and city

- **City:** **Poznań** (~100k students, dense campus clusters).
- **When:** **last week of September to mid-October** (start of the academic year on 1 October, plus welcome weeks). Refresh the flyers once, in week 2, because boards get covered fast. Reuse the kit in the next city at the start of its summer semester (late February).

## 8. Budget

**600–900 PLN total (runs in month 1)**

| Item | PLN |
|---|---|
| Printing: 400× A4 tear-off flyers, 100× A3 posters | 300–400 |
| Stickers with QR (1,000 pcs) | 150–250 |
| Small boost of the TikTok/IG post to students in Poznań (optional) | 150–250 |
| Distribution | 0 (the team plus 2–3 volunteer student ambassadors) |

## 9. KPIs

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| **Primary: QR scans → installs** | 1,200 scans → 300–450 installs |
| Map sessions with ≥ 3 event detail opens in the first session | ≥ 35% of campaign users |
| D7 retention (weekend return) | ≥ 20% |

## 10. Risks and mitigation

- **Most likely failure:** the flyers get torn down or covered within 48 hours, or the administration removes them for not being approved, and scans stay near zero.
- **Mitigation:** get written permission (email is enough) from dorm administrations and the student government **before** printing, and ask them to put the flyer on their official board. Use per-location QR codes to find the 5–10 spots that work and re-post only there. Keep the student government newsletter and first-year Facebook groups as the digital backup channel.
