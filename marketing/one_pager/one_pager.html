The Solution

Wydarzka puts your venue on a live, interactive map — visible to every user in your city who's looking for something to do right now.

Your venue is already on our map

We aggregate public event data from across the web. Just claim your profile for free — verify with a quick SMS or email, and you're in control.

Create and publish events in minutes

Push-notify your followers directly
