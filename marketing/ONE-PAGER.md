### The Solution

**Wydarzka** is an event discovery app that puts your venue on a live, interactive map — visible to every user in your city who's looking for something to do **right now.**

Here's how it works:

1. **Your venue is already on our map.** We aggregate public event data from across the web. You just **claim your profile for free** — verify with a quick SMS or email, and you're in control.
2. **Create and publish events in minutes.** A simple dashboard lets you add events, upload photos, set prices, and link to your ticket page. No design skills needed.
3. **Send a push notification to your followers.** One tap, and everyone who follows your venue gets a direct notification on their phone. No algorithm. No pay-to-play. **They see it. Period.**

### Why It Works

- **More people through your door.** Your events are discoverable by thousands of locals and visitors browsing the map — people who are actively looking for plans, not passively scrolling a feed.
- **Zero marketing budget required.** No ads to buy, no posts to boost. Wydarzka's push notifications reach your followers directly — **for free.** You get the reach of a marketing campaign with the effort of pressing a button.
- **Know what's actually working.** See exactly how many people viewed your event, clicked "Buy tickets," or tapped "Navigate" to your venue. Real numbers, not vanity metrics.
