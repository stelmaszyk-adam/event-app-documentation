# Wydarzka — One-Pager for Venues & Event Organizers (B2B)

> **Status:** validation version, Poznań, 15.10.2026. English master of [walidacja-poznan/ONE-PAGER-PL.md](./walidacja-poznan/ONE-PAGER-PL.md) — **the Polish file is the one that gets printed; change both together and diff them before every print run.**
> **How it is used:** handed over **after** a conversation ([SKRYPT-ROZMOWY.md](./walidacja-poznan/SKRYPT-ROZMOWY.md) §2 — only at pain ≥ 4 and after consent to be contacted). It is a leave-behind, not a cold flyer.
> **Rules this text follows** ([KAMPANIA-POZNAN.md](./KAMPANIA-POZNAN.md) §2): one city, no "thousands of users", no "free forever", no Pro price, no feature we cannot show on a phone during the conversation. The promise must be smaller than what we deliver.
> **Format:** A5, two-sided. Front = pain, proof and the offer. Back = what it looks like in numbers and the single ask. Sections marked *(internal)* are not printed.

Quiz on Tuesday, stand-up on Thursday, a vernissage on Saturday — and a Facebook post that a handful of followers will see, because that is what the algorithm decided. People in Poznań are looking for something to do tonight, and they keep landing on the same big concerts from ticket portals. The small, cheap and free events disappear.

**[QR → `wydarzka.dev/poznan/venue/<id>`]** — scan it, that is your listing as of today.

We created it ourselves, from publicly available sources (your website, your profiles, event portals). **It is yours and you can take it over — through us today, with your own login after 20 November:**

- Something wrong — name, address, opening hours, photo? Tell us and we fix it the same day.

- Don't want to be there, or don't want a particular photo? We take it down immediately, no questions.

### What you get out of it

- **People who do not follow you see you.** A map of Poznań on wydarzka.dev plus the weekly guide **"Co robić w Poznaniu w ten weekend"** — newsletter on Thursday morning, Instagram carousel, TikTok.
- **A permanent event page** instead of a post that sinks after two days — with address, time, ticket link and navigation.
- **An English edition** — Erasmus students and foreigners your Polish Facebook post never reaches.
- **We tag you** in every carousel and every story. That comes back to you as followers on your own Instagram.
- **A ready-made event graphic** (square and stories) — yours, to use anywhere.
- **Numbers every Monday:** how many people saw the event, how many tapped "Tickets", how many tapped "Navigate". A boosted Facebook post costs 50–100 PLN to reach strangers. This costs you nothing — and you know what you got for it.

### How it works — zero admin on your side

1. **You send us your programme** — a photo of the poster on WhatsApp, a link to your post, or a short form. Nothing to install, no account to manage.
2. **We put the events on the map** and pick the best ones for the newsletter and Instagram. We make the graphic ourselves — we do not repost other people's posters.
3. **On Monday you get a report** with last week's numbers.

## Notes *(internal — do not print)*

### What this version does differently from the September one

- **"You are already on the map" moved to the front**, not the back. It is the only hard proof we have before issue #1 — and the only moment the person reaches for their own phone.
- **The takeover is described as something a human does, not a form.** The B2B dashboard is unbuilt ([ROADMAP-web-b2b.md](../ROADMAP-web-b2b.md) §2.1 and [ROADMAP-backend.md](../ROADMAP-backend.md) §2.1 — all `- [ ]`), and SMS verification could not work today anyway because the database holds **0 phone numbers** ([PLAN-30-DNI.md](./walidacja-poznan/PLAN-30-DNI.md) §2). Hence "through us today, with your own login after 20.11".
- **Benefits are stated as the venue's outcome, not our activity.** "A listing on the map" → "people who do not follow you see you".
- **A cost anchor without quoting our price** (50–100 PLN per boost vs. 0 PLN) — it lets the venue price us themselves before the paid test in week 4.
- **"Zero admin" stays**, because that is hypothesis H2 under test ([PLAN-30-DNI.md](./walidacja-poznan/PLAN-30-DNI.md) §1), but it is now paired with "what changes once the dashboard launches" — so the partner does not get used to a concierge and then read the dashboard as a chore.
- **The back side now exists in Polish too.** The September PL file stopped at "let's talk": no sample report, no FAQ, no next step, no data-controller line.

### Per-venue QR rule — mandatory

Use a personalised QR **only for venues whose listing we reviewed by hand the day before.** The database currently holds duplicates (Teatr Wielki ×3, Klub Tama ×3), 148/177 venues with no category, junk names from eBilet and seed data ([PLAN-30-DNI.md](./walidacja-poznan/PLAN-30-DNI.md) §2). Showing an owner a tripled or uncategorised listing turns our strongest proof into an accusation. Generic prints get a blank square for a QR sticker.

The route is by `id`, not slug, and there is no `pl` prefix (`localePrefix: "as-needed"`): **`wydarzka.dev/poznan/venue/<id>`**. The `…/poznan/venues/<slug>` form used in the previous version does not exist and would 404.
