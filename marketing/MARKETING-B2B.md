# Wydarzka — B2B Marketing Plan (Venue Acquisition)

> **Goal:** Acquire 200 claimed venues within 3 months of launch across 7 Polish cities.
> **Target audience:** Venue owners, event organizers, bar/club managers, cultural center directors, restaurant owners hosting events.

---

## 1. Value Proposition for Venues

### Core messaging (PL primary, EN secondary)

| Pain Point | Wydarzka Solution |
|---|---|
| Events scattered across Facebook, Instagram, own website — no single source of truth | One dashboard to publish events, visible on map to thousands of local users |
| No direct communication channel with attendees | Free push notifications to venue followers (1/day) |
| No visibility into event performance | Analytics: views, ticket clicks, navigation clicks, follower growth |
| Claiming and managing presence is complex | 5-minute claim flow (SMS/email verification), guided onboarding |
| Social media graphics take time to produce | Auto-generated event graphics for Instagram (P2 feature, mention as upcoming) |

### Elevator pitch (PL)

> "Wydarzka to darmowa platforma, dzieki ktorej Twoje wydarzenia trafiaja na interaktywna mape miasta. Zarzadzaj wydarzeniami z jednego panelu, wysylaj powiadomienia push do obserwujacych i sledz statystyki — bez oplat, bez prowizji."

### Elevator pitch (EN)

> "Wydarzka is a free platform that puts your events on an interactive city map. Manage events from one dashboard, send push notifications to followers, and track analytics — no fees, no commissions."

## 2. Launch Strategy — City-by-City Rollout

### 2.1 Pre-launch (Weeks 1-4 before city launch)

| Action | Channel | Owner | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seed the map with aggregated events | Aggregation pipeline | Backend/Ops | 1,000+ events from public sources before any outreach |
| Identify top 50 venues per city | Manual research | Marketing | Prioritize: high event frequency, active social media, 100+ followers |
| Prepare personalized outreach list | CRM / Spreadsheet | Marketing | Venue name, contact person, email, phone, social links, event frequency |

### 2.2 City launch order (suggested)

1. **Krakow** — dense nightlife/culture scene, student population, high event frequency
2. **Wroclaw** — strong cultural scene, tech-savvy audience
3. **Warszawa** — largest market, highest competition, launch after proving model
4. **Gdansk/Trojmiasto** — summer events, tourism
5. **Poznan** — business events, student scene
6. **Katowice** — music/club scene, Silesia region hub
7. **Lodz** — cultural revitalization, growing event scene

## 3. Acquisition Channels

### 3.1 Direct Outreach (Primary — highest conversion expected)

**Target:** 50 venues per city, personalized approach

#### Email outreach sequence

| Email | Timing | Subject (PL) | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| #1 Introduction | Day 0 | "Twoje wydarzenia na mapie [City] — za darmo" | Value prop + screenshot of their venue already on map (from aggregated data) |
| #2 Follow-up | Day 3 | "Widzielismy [Event Name] — swietna frekwencja!" | Reference a specific recent event, show how it looks on Wydarzka map |
| #3 Social proof | Day 7 | "[Competitor Venue] juz jest na Wydarzka" | Show early adopters in their city/category |
| #4 Final | Day 14 | "Ostatnia wiadomosc — darmowy panel czeka" | FOMO + direct link to claim page |

#### Phone/in-person outreach

- Visit venues during off-hours (Tuesday-Thursday afternoons)
- Bring a tablet with the app showing their venue on the map
- Offer to complete the claim process together on the spot (5 min)
- Leave a one-pager (see 3.5)

#### Instagram/Facebook DM outreach

- Follow venue's social profiles
- Engage with their event posts (genuine comments)
- DM after 2-3 interactions: "Hej! Widzialem wasze wydarzenia — macie swietny program. Chcieliscie byc na interaktywnej mapie wydarzen w [City]?"

## 4. Onboarding & Activation

### 4.1 Claim flow optimization

- **Pre-populated venue data** — venue is already on the map from aggregation; organizer claims, not creates from scratch
- **5-minute claim process** — SMS verification as primary (fastest)
- **Guided onboarding checklist** after claim (see ROADMAP-web-b2b.md 2.2):
  1. Add venue photo
  2. Complete description
  3. Add opening hours
  4. Add first event
  5. Send push to followers

### 4.2 White-glove onboarding (first 20 venues per city)

- Dedicated onboarding call (15 min) or in-person visit
- Help upload photos, write description, create first event
- Send first push notification together (demonstrate value immediately)
- Check in after 1 week: "How's it going? Need help with anything?"

### 4.3 Activation triggers

| Trigger | Action | Channel |
|---|---|---|
| Venue claimed but no event after 3 days | "Create your first event in 2 minutes" email | Email |
| Event created but not published after 2 days | "Your event is ready — publish it now" push/email | Email + in-app |
| Event published but no push sent | "X followers are waiting — notify them!" in-dashboard prompt | In-app |
| No login for 7 days | "You have X new followers this week" email | Email |
| Follower milestone (10, 50, 100) | Congratulations email with analytics snapshot | Email + in-app |

## 9. Messaging Templates

### 9.1 Claim landing page headline

> **PL:** "Twoje miejsce juz jest na mapie. Przejmij kontrole — za darmo."
> **EN:** "Your venue is already on the map. Take control — for free."

### 9.2 Key objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We already use Facebook Events" | "Wydarzka shows your events to people who don't follow you on Facebook — they discover you on the map by location and category." |
| "We don't have time for another platform" | "5 minutes to claim. After that, one event form replaces posting across 3 platforms. Plus push notifications your followers actually see (not buried in algorithm)." |
| "What does it cost?" | "Completely free. No commission, no subscription, no hidden fees. We monetize through promoted placements (optional, coming later)." |
| "How many users do you have?" | "X,000 events already on the map in [city]. Y users discovering events daily. Your competitors [Venue A, B] are already listed." |
| "We'll think about it" | "The claim takes 5 minutes and your venue is already on the map from public data. By claiming, you get control over how it appears + push notifications to followers." |

## 10. Competitive Positioning

| Competitor | Their weakness | Our advantage |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook Events | Algorithm suppresses reach, no push notifications, discovery limited to social graph | Map-based discovery, guaranteed push delivery, no algorithm |
| Google Maps / Business | No event management, no push, no analytics for events | Purpose-built for events, push notifications, event analytics |
| Eventbrite / Going | Commission on tickets, focused on paid events | Free for all events (free + paid), no commission |
| City portals (e.g., karnet.krakow.pl) | No organizer dashboard, read-only listings, outdated data | Self-service dashboard, real-time updates, push notifications |
